Aerodynamics

Fundamental and Applied Aerodynamics

This section contains study notes on the theoretical foundations and fundamental concepts of aerodynamics.

The topics cover governing equations, compressible flow, viscous flow and boundary layers, turbulence, and hypersonic flow.

Contents
  • Fundamentals of Aerodynamics Mathematical foundations and governing equations
  • Compressible Flow Isentropic flow, shock waves, and expansion waves
  • Viscous Flow and Boundary Layers Viscous effects, boundary layers, and flow separation
  • Turbulence Turbulent flow, Reynolds averaging, and turbulence modeling
  • Hypersonic Flow High-speed flow and hypersonic aerodynamic phenomena
Overview

Aerodynamics is the study of fluid motion and the forces generated by the interaction between a fluid flow and solid bodies.

These notes are organized from the mathematical foundations and conservation laws to compressible, viscous, turbulent, and high-speed aerodynamic flows.
